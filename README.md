# leakyleft

**Your data. Their incident. Our paperwork.**

A fictional copyleft license for personal data that has already escaped.
Because apparently the only thing missing from a data breach was a license file.

[Read the license](LICENSE)

> This repository is technical satire. Its license is fictional and claims no
> legal force. This repository contains no real personal data. Please keep it
> that way.

## The premise

Someone leaks your personal information. The copies spread. A committee is
formed. After careful consideration, it recommends adding a `LICENSE` file.

Leakyleft imagines a copyleft regime in which every copy of a leaked record must
carry the original notice, a complete chain of institutional excuses, and the
corresponding remorse. Accountability propagates downstream. In theory.

The spreadsheet, meanwhile, has been forwarded again.

The joke is aimed at organizations that substitute paperwork for protection,
and at the fantasy that a licensing trick can put consent back into an export.
People affected by breaches are not the punchline.

## Read the fine print

The full **Leakyleft Public License, version 0.0.0 — Too Late** is in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Among its fictional requirements:

- Preserve the chain of regret with every redistributed copy.
- Provide complete corresponding remorse, including the apology's editable source.
- Credit the decisions that caused the incident, rather than the person it harmed.
- Restore privacy by returning every copy to a time before the leak.

No installation required. The incident has already been deployed.
