**You violated my privacy. Please don't violate my license too.**

A fictional copyleft-style license addressed to anyone who copies, spreads,
remixes, or exploits my personal information without asking.

If you're going to treat my private life like an open repository, at least
pretend to respect its `LICENSE`.

[Read the license](LICENSE)

> This is satire, not consent. Leakyleft grants no permission to publish or use
> anyone's personal data, creates no legal rights or enforceable obligations,
> and claims no legal force. This repository contains no real personal data.
> Please keep it that way.

## The premise

My personal information was never an open-source project. Apparently, someone
forked it anyway.

Leakyleft is my imaginary response: if you insist on copying, redistributing,
combining, profiling, training on, or otherwise misusing my data without
permission, take the copyleft-shaped terms with you.

Copies inherit the notice. Remixes inherit the terms. Anyone sharing my data
gets to imagine their own data being shared under exactly the same conditions.
Suddenly, everyone has strong opinions about consent.

The contradiction is deliberate: this "license" talks about permissions I never
granted. Following its fictional terms cannot turn a privacy violation into
consent. The first-person narrator and all data in the scenario are imaginary.

## Read the fine print

The **Leakyleft Public License, version 0.0.0 — Too Late** is in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Its fictional terms include:

- **Redistribution:** carry the notice and these terms with every copy, forward,
  mirror, and resale. "Just forwarding it" is still distribution with a smaller
  job title.
- **Derivative Works:** aggregation, enrichment, profiling, inferred attributes,
  and AI training inherit the same terms. Turning my life into a model does not
  make it your original content.
- **Corresponding Source:** disclose where the data came from and every step of
  its acquisition and processing. "Found it online" is not a reproducible build.
  The joke asks for an imaginary audit trail, not more real personal data.
- **ShareAlike:** if my privacy is fair game, imagine yours under the same terms.
  No special exception for the person holding the spreadsheet. This is fictional
  symmetry, not an invitation to expose anyone's information.
- **Termination:** your permissions terminate immediately. This is unusually
  efficient because you never had any.

No installation required. Someone already ran `git clone` on my private life.
